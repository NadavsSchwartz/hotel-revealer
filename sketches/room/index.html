The practical details

Before
you book.

The useful part is knowing what the evidence tells you—and where it stops.

Will I know exactly which hotel I’ll get?

No. We compare listing clues to suggest possible hotels. Even when the available details line up, the hotel’s identity remains unverified.

What does a partial candidate mean?

Some clues support the comparison, while others are missing or cannot be assessed. A missing amenity doesn’t mean the hotel lacks it. It means we don’t have enough evidence to use that clue.

Do I book my stay here?

You explore and compare here. Booking takes place on Priceline, where you’ll check the final total, room details, dates, and terms before paying.

What if there isn’t a convincing match?

We don’t force one. Some offers have too little evidence, and the retrieved listings may be incomplete. You can still review the original offer, with its uncertainty in mind.

Are the price and savings guaranteed?

No. Rates and availability can change. A separate retail quote may also describe a different room or booking policy. Always check taxes, fees, and the final total on the original offer.

Are you part of Priceline?

No. Hotel Revealer is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Priceline. Priceline supplies the original offers and handles reservations.
