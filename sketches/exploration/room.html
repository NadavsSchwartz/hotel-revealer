A note on certainty

A possibility.
Never a promise.

Priceline Express offers leave the hotel unnamed until after booking. Hotel Revealer compares offer details with possible hotels to help you consider your options.

It cannot confirm the hotel’s identity. Similar properties can share the same details. Check the booking terms and make your decision with that uncertainty in mind.
