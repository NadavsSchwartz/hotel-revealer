A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little clarity
before you commit.

Is the hotel’s identity confirmed?

No. Matching clues suggest a possible hotel. They don’t establish its identity. You should be comfortable with the original unnamed offer before booking.

Where do I make the booking?

On Priceline. Check the final total, dates, room, cancellation policy, taxes, and fees there. Hotel Revealer is an independent comparison project and is not affiliated with Priceline.

What happens when evidence is missing?

It stays missing. A partial candidate has less supporting evidence, and some offers have no supported candidate. Retrieved hotel listings can also be incomplete. We don’t force a match.

Can the rate change?

Yes. Availability and rates can change. A named hotel’s retail quote may also describe a different room or policy. Always check the original offer before paying.
